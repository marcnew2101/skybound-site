SKYBOUND

Stack your way to the stars

Support

Skybound is a block-stacking game for iPhone. Tap to drop each block from the swinging crane, land it square, and build as high as you can while the sky changes around you.

Questions, bugs, or ideas? Email marcnew2101@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Frequently asked

How do I earn Straighten Up and Second Chance?
Both charge from PERFECT landings over a run. Straighten Up charges every 10, Second Chance every 20. The buttons at the bottom corners show when one is ready.
What does "Your City" do?
Any run of 5 floors or more adds its tower to the skyline behind your next game. The city keeps your 10 best towers.
Where are my scores stored?
On your device. If you're signed in to Game Center, your best score and tallest tower are also sent to its leaderboards.
How do I turn the music or sound off?
Tap the gear in the top-left corner. Music, sound effects, haptics, and the theme are all there.
