PRIVACY POLICY

Thenpen LLC and its games, including Skybound Towers

Effective November 6, 2026

The short version

Our games do not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your scores, your city of towers, and your settings (sound, music, haptics, theme, and which tips you've seen) are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere by the game. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Skybound Towers sends your best score and tallest tower to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and reports achievements you unlock. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The game never sees your Game Center friends, contacts, or any profile details beyond what Apple shows inside its own interface. You can play without signing in; the game works the same.

Support messages

If you write to us through the support form, we receive what you type, including your email address if you give it, and use it only to reply. We do not add you to any list.

Children

Our games are suitable for all ages and collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the support form.
