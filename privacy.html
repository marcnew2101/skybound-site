SKYBOUND

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

The short version

Skybound does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your scores, your city of towers, and your settings (sound, music, haptics, theme, and which tips you've seen) are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere by Skybound. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Skybound sends your best score and tallest tower to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and reports achievements you unlock. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Skybound never sees your Game Center friends, contacts, or any profile details beyond what Apple shows inside its own interface. You can play without signing in; the game works the same.

Children

Skybound is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
